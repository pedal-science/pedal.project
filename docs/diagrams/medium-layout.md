# Medium pedal: internal layout, top down

`medium-layout.svg` is a 2D layout draft of the 1590DD-size pedal with the **front (player)
at the bottom** and the rear at the top. `medium-layout.png` is a screen preview and
`medium-layout.pdf` is a 1:1 vector copy (300 × 218 mm page; the case itself is 178.26 mm
wide, so print at 100%).

It is a **layout draft, not a drilling template**. The cutout and switch positions are the
owner's; the parts around them are placed to those constraints.

## What is fixed

| Item | Value | Source |

| Case interior | 178.26 × 110.26 mm, ~33 mm high | owner |
| Enclosure screws | 6: four corners + top/bottom centres; full-height aluminium | owner |
| Footswitches | 69 mm pitch, centres 29 mm from the bottom edge; middle on the centre line | owner |
| Pi 5 | 85 × 56 mm landscape, M2.5 holes at 58 × 49 | Raspberry Pi |
| Codec | SparkFun WM8960, 25.4 × 40.64 mm | `enclosure/assets/parts/parts.json` |
| Pico 2 W | 51 × 21 mm; 21 mm dimension vertical, on a carrier ~55 × 8 mm in plan; moved up 8 mm to clear the bottom-centre pillar | owner |
| Output board | 15 × 15 mm, one per output | owner |
| Input jack | 6.35 mm, top edge | owner |
| Output jack | 6.35 mm, top edge, left of the input | owner |

## What is assumed, and worth checking

- **Screen cutout.** 99 × 59 mm, 10 mm from the left and 9 mm from the top, as given.
  No screen-module outline is dimensioned, so the drawing shows the cutout exactly and the
  Pi behind it. The 9 mm strip between the cutout and the top edge is free for the Pi's
  USB-C/HDMI leads; the Pi's top edge sits 1.5 mm below the cutout top.
- **Pi.** 85 × 56 mm landscape, centred behind the cutout on the screen's Pi standoffs
  (x = 16.5..101.5 mm, y = 10.5..66.5 mm). USB-C/HDMI on the top edge, USB-A/Ethernet on
  the right edge, 40-pin header along the bottom edge. Its bottom edge is clear of the
  footswitches. Check the exact connector positions against your board.
- **Screw pillars.** Six of them: four corners plus the centres of the top and bottom edges.
  They are aluminium from the inside ceiling to the floor, so nothing can sit under them. The
  drawing hatches all six; the section shows the bottom-centre one full height. The Pico is
  moved up by 8 mm (the pillar width) from its draft position, leaving about 0.8 mm between
  its bottom edge and the pillar.
- **Input and output jacks.** Both on the top edge, output at x=120 and input at x=158
  (about 38 mm apart). The input jack is placed clear of the top-right lid boss by about
  1 mm; check the boss diameter on your case.
- **Codec.** Between the two jacks, FS3, the Pi and the right wall: x=128..153,
  y=20..61. The input lead is under 20 mm. The output jack is close to the codec in plan
  because both are on the top edge; the output *board* is kept at the front-left, away from
  the input wiring.

## Regenerating

The drawing is generated by a plain-Python script, so dimensions and positions are edited in
one place:

```bash
python3 enclosure/src/medium_layout.py
rsvg-convert -w 1600 docs/diagrams/medium-layout.svg -o docs/diagrams/medium-layout.png
rsvg-convert -f pdf -w 1134 -h 824 docs/diagrams/medium-layout.svg -o docs/diagrams/medium-layout.pdf
```

Coordinates in the script are the case's internal frame: `x = 0` at the left wall, `y = 0`
at the rear wall, `y` increasing toward the player. The SVG itself is in millimetres
(1 user unit = 1 mm), with a 50 mm scale bar.
